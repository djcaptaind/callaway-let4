LET 4 • WEEK 2 • LESSON 2

Decisions Define Leaders.

Senior cadets examine decisions through an organizational-leadership lens, including stakeholders, communication, risk, accountability, second-order consequences, and team performance.

Analyze

Identify the problem, facts, stakeholders, and values involved.

Evaluate

Compare choices, risks, consequences, and effects on trust.

Decide

Select and defend a responsible course of action.

ESSENTIAL QUESTION

How should senior leaders make decisions that protect standards, develop people, and strengthen the organization?

LEARNING OBJECTIVE

Evaluate complex leadership situations, select and defend an ethical course of action, communicate the decision, assign responsibility, anticipate consequences, and identify how success will be measured.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the End of the Lesson

  • Identify stakeholders, standards, risks, and constraints.
  • Compare immediate and second-order consequences.
  • Develop a leadership communication plan.
  • Assign ownership and establish follow-up controls.
  • Defend decisions using values, evidence, and judgment.
  • Evaluate effects on culture and trust.
