Course Overview

Meet Your Course

LET 3 focuses on leadership in action. You will practice communication, teamwork, decision-making, responsibility, citizenship, and service.

L

Leadership

Lead yourself and others with purpose.

C

Communication

Speak, listen, write, and present effectively.

T

Build trust and contribute to group success.

R

Responsibility

Own your decisions, preparation, and conduct.

S

Service

Use leadership to improve your community.

Prepare for college, careers, and adult life.

Leadership Spotlight

Leadership Begins Before the Title

Leadership is visible in preparation, attitude, reliability, and the willingness to act when something needs to be done.

A position can give someone authority, but daily choices build credibility. In LET 3, leadership will be measured by how consistently you help the team succeed.
