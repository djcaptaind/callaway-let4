Core Idea

What Is Leadership?

Leadership is the ability to influence people toward a shared purpose while accepting responsibility for your actions and the well-being of the team.

Leadership is not limited to rank, position, age, or personality. A person can lead by setting an example, helping the group stay focused, listening carefully, solving problems, encouraging others, or taking responsibility when something goes wrong.

InfluenceYour actions affect how others think, feel, and respond.
PurposeLeadership helps a team move toward a clear goal.
ResponsibilityLeaders own their choices and their impact on others.
ServiceLeadership includes helping others and strengthening the team.
TrustPeople follow leaders who are reliable, honest, and consistent.
GrowthLeadership improves through reflection, feedback, and practice.
Leadership in Action

Effective Leadership Behaviors

  • 1Lead by example: demonstrate the standards you expect from others.
  • 2Communicate clearly: listen, ask questions, and explain expectations respectfully.
  • 3Take initiative: act responsibly when something needs to be done.
  • 4Support the team: help others improve without embarrassing or disrespecting them.
  • 5Make sound decisions: consider the goal, the people involved, and the possible consequences.
  • 6Accept responsibility: acknowledge mistakes and take corrective action.
