Student Success

What Success Looks Like

Leadership begins before a person receives a title. Preparation, reliability, initiative, respect, and follow-through build credibility.
  • ✓Check Canvas and class announcements regularly.
  • ✓Arrive prepared and participate productively.
  • ✓Ask questions and seek clarification when needed.
  • ✓Respect classmates, instructors, equipment, and the learning environment.
  • ✓Lead by example through daily choices.
