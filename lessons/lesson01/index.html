FOUNDATIONAL LEADERSHIP

Leadership Is a Choice.

Senior leaders are judged not only by what they do, but by the standards, trust, and culture their decisions create for everyone who follows.

45–60 minutes Interactive lesson Progress saved
ESSENTIAL QUESTION

How does a senior cadet deliberately shape standards, trust, and team culture?

PART 1

Leadership Expands Beyond the Title

LET 4 leadership requires more than personal example. Senior cadets must establish expectations, develop others, communicate clearly, and protect the long-term health of the team.

01

Choice

You decide which standard to establish, how to communicate it, and who must be developed to sustain it.

02

Influence

Your decisions shape team climate, informal norms, trust, and the behavior junior cadets believe leaders will accept.

03

Responsibility

Senior leaders identify ownership, delegate responsibility, follow up, and remain accountable for the final result.

04

Service

Strong leadership develops people, strengthens systems, and leaves the organization more capable than before.

KEY IDEA

Senior leadership is visible in the standards, systems, and people a leader develops consistently.

Your Leadership Compass

I apply standards consistently, even when doing so is inconvenient or unpopular.
I protect dignity while still communicating clear expectations and corrective feedback.
I address difficult issues early and accept the risk of responsible leadership.
I establish ownership, delegate clearly, follow up, and accept responsibility for the team's outcome.
I develop other leaders and place organizational success above personal recognition.
